01 — Who I Am

About Me

I am a third-year IT student passionate about web development and technology. I enjoy turning ideas into interactive websites using HTML, CSS, JavaScript, and modern frameworks. My goal is to become a skilled Full-Stack Developer by continuously learning, building meaningful projects, and solving real-world problems.

Beyond the classroom, I've built an online voting system, an enrollment system, and a management dashboard for a local water refilling station. Each one taught me how to turn real requirements into clean, working software. I also sharpen my UI skills in Figma and explore data and networking through my Cisco coursework.
